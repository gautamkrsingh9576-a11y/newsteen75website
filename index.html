What NewsTeen75 Does

NewsTeen75 is a news aggregator, not an original reporting newsroom. The application organizes publicly available news links, headlines, and short-form video updates into topic feeds for convenient discovery on Android devices.

How Users Discover News

Readers can browse latest stories, filter topics by interest (such as India, World, Technology, Business, Sports, and Entertainment), watch short news videos, and bookmark articles for later reading.

Articles and videos surfaced through NewsTeen75 originate from third-party publishers. Original publishers retain responsibility and rights for their respective reporting.

Sourcing & Attribution Policy

News Sources & Content Transparency

NewsTeen75 (com.newsteen75.app) operates as a news aggregation application. Below is a clear explanation of how third-party content is surfaced, attributed, and linked.

1. How News Content Is Discovered

NewsTeen75 aggregates publicly available news headlines, summaries, links, and short video updates from third-party publishers across categories such as India, World, Technology, Business, Sports, and Entertainment.

2. Original Publishers and Authors

NewsTeen75 does not claim ownership over third-party articles. Original publishers and authors retain full responsibility for their respective reporting, editorial standards, and intellectual property rights, as applicable.

3. External News Links

Aggregated items provide clear attribution and links allowing readers to access the full article directly on the original publisher's website.

4. Publication Dates

Where provided by the source feed, publication dates or timestamps are displayed alongside stories so readers understand the recency of each item.

5. Third-Party Videos

Short-form news videos accessible within the application are surfaced from third-party sources and remain subject to the rights and policies of their respective creators and platforms.

6. Content Correction Requests

Publishers or readers who identify missing attribution, broken source links, or content concerns can contact us directly for review or removal from the aggregation feed.
